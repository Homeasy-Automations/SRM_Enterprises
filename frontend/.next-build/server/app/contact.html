Help Us Understand Your Requirement

A few additional details help our engineering team recommend the right protective packaging and quote the best volume rates faster.

Product

What are you packing?

Electronic PCB, automotive casting, fragile glassware, pharmaceutical vials, or retail goods.

Dimensions

Length × Width × Height

Internal cavity size or outer carton dimensions in mm, cm or inches.

Material

Corrugated / Foam / Bubble / Poly

Preferred material grade: 3/5/7 ply, EPE density, bubble GSM, or LDPE thickness.

Quantity

Trial / Regular / Bulk

Target sample count, one-off production run, or monthly recurring scheduled dispatches.

Application

Storage / Transit / Export

Warehouse stacking height, sea freight export moisture protection, or drop-test compliance.

Reference

Photos / drawings / existing packaging

CAD 2D/3D models, component photos, or existing carton benchmark photos.

Don't have all the technical details? That's totally okay.

Just tell us what you're trying to pack and roughly how much it weighs. Our technical packaging engineers will calculate the required flute, burst strength, and foam densities for you.
